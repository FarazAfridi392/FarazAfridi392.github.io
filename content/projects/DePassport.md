DePassport is an AI-powered compatibility platform that connects people for dating, mentorship, and fitness partnerships through intelligent matching scores. The app combines optimized user interfaces and deep navigation flows with client-side caching for faster content loading. It also integrates cross-platform payments through Stripe, Apple Pay, and Google Pay, supported by low-latency API integrations.
