Luminate is a telehealth appointment platform that enables patients to book video consultations based on doctors' available time slots. It features reusable booking and slot-selection interfaces for browsing doctor availability, secure video consultation workflows for patient–doctor sessions, and REST API integrations for managing doctor and patient schedules. The application follows Clean Architecture with GetX to ensure maintainable and testable modules, while Jenkins CI/CD automates builds, testing, and deployment.
