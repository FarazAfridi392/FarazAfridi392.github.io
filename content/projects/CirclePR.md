CirclePR is a corporate gifting automation platform that helps businesses streamline employee and client gifting through smart catalogs and integrated payments. The application includes automated gifting workflows, catalog segmentation, reusable UI components, and admin tools for managing catalogs, orders, and users. Prefetching and client-side caching improved feed loading speed by 40%, while Stripe, Apple Pay, and Google Pay support cross-platform payments.
