Riderem is a multi-restaurant food delivery marketplace connecting customers, restaurants, and riders through synchronized applications powered by shared backend services. It includes real-time order tracking, rider assignment workflows, and Google Maps integration for live location tracking, routing, and ETA calculation. Its data models and shared services support multiple restaurant operations and a consistent experience across the customer, restaurant, and rider apps.
